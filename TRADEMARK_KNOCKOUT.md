# Trademark Knockout Search — Results (preliminary)

> **Preliminary knockout only — not a legal clearance opinion.** Based on public web + app-store + basic trademark-directory searches (July 2026), not a full USPTO TESS/design-mark or attorney search. App-store presence is *common-law* use (a real conflict risk) even without a federal registration. Get an attorney opinion before filing/committing.

## Bottom line

**None of the five current names is clean.** Two are live, same-category competitor apps (disqualifying), one is heavily crowded, the lead name "Twinit" is already an active software company with the domain taken, and the least-conflicted option is a weak/descriptive mark. **Recommendation: run a fresh round of coined/fanciful names** (which are both more available and far stronger trademarks) and knockout-screen those.

## What this means

The pattern is clear: **descriptive roots (fit-, wardrobe-, twin-, mimic) are all taken** in this space, because everyone reaches for the same obvious words. The two "closest to the product" names (Fitroom, Wardro) are exactly the ones already occupied by shipping competitors. This is the same lesson as "Drobe."

The fix is to go **coined/fanciful** — an invented or unexpected word with no descriptive meaning in fashion. These are (a) far more likely to be available and (b) the *strongest* category of trademark (think "Depop," "Vinted," "Klarna," "Bumble" — made-up or borrowed-from-elsewhere words). Descriptive names are both crowded and legally weak.

## Recommended next steps

1. **Treat Fitroom, Wardro, and Mimic as dead.** Don't build on them.
2. **Twinit:** only viable with a proper attorney clearance in Class 9 (and accepting the software-space "Twinit" already exists + the .com is gone). Reasonable to keep as a *maybe*, not a lock.
3. **Generate a fresh shortlist of ~10 coined names**, then knockout-screen each (USPTO TESS, App Store/Play, `@handle`, domain) and keep only those that are clear on all four.
4. **Then** get a trademark attorney to do a full clearance on the top 1–2 survivors before filing the Intent-to-Use (Classes 9 + 42) from `TRADEMARK_CLEARANCE.md`.
5. Whatever wins, **grab the domain + `@handle` + App Store/Play name the same day** — those go first-come.

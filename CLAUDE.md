## Vault (obsidian-mind)

An `om` MCP server exposes Ben's obsidian-mind vault to this repo. It holds prior
decisions, rejected approaches, business constraints, and compliance rules that are
not recorded anywhere in this codebase.

**Before proposing an architecture, a dependency, or a design that affects how this
project works, search the vault.** Use `search` for topics, `recall` for lessons
scoped to this repo, and `expand` to follow a note's links. If the vault has already
recorded a decision, follow it or say explicitly why you are departing from it.

Do not implement an approach the vault records as rejected.

Usage

Do & don't

Do

  • Keep it lowercase, always.
  • Preserve clear space ≥ the height of the "s" around the mark.
  • Keep the reflection fading to transparent (never a hard mirror).
  • Use the reflected-dot icon below ~24px wordmark height.

Don't

  • Don't capitalize or add a period ("Selv." / "SELV").
  • Don't recolor outside the palette or add outlines/shadows.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or space out the letters.
  • Don't put the wordmark on a busy photo without a scrim.

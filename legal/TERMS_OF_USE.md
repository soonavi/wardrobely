## 2. What Selv is — and what it is not

Selv is a virtual wardrobe and try-on app. In this version, you can:

- **Design a character** in an in-app character creator — skin tone, face shape, eyes, eyebrows, hair, facial hair, body type, and accessories, all picked from preset option catalogs.
- **Digitize your closet** by photographing clothes you own and adding a name, color, brand, category, and tags.
- **Browse a partner catalog** of items from brands we work with.
- **Try items on your character**, build outfits from what you own and what you're considering, and save those outfits with names.
- **Save a share card** — a branded image of your character in an outfit — to your camera roll, or hand it to your phone's share sheet.
- **Click out to a retailer** to buy a catalog item (§9).

Two things Selv is explicitly **not**, and you should not rely on it as if it were:

- **Not a fit or sizing service.** The try-on renders a stylized character wearing a stylized garment. It is an appearance preview, not a measurement simulation, and it does not predict whether a given size will fit you. Sizes shown on a product page are an availability list, not a picker — you choose your size on the retailer's own page at checkout. Do not treat anything in Selv as sizing advice.
- **Not a body-measurement or body-scanning product.** Your character is built entirely from preset choices you pick. We do not ask for, take, or process a photo or scan of your face or body to build it. Height and weight are optional profile fields you may leave blank; they are reference-only and do not shape your character. See `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` §4.

We may change, add, or remove features at any time (§18).

## 9. Shopping through Selv: affiliate links and third-party retailers

This is the section most worth reading carefully, because who you are contracting with is not obvious from the app.

**You buy from the retailer, not from Selv.** When you tap "Buy at [brand]," Selv opens that brand's own product page in your device's browser. Everything after that happens between you and the retailer:

| Who does what | Selv | The retailer |
|---|---|---|
| Shows you the item | ✅ | ✅ |
| Sells the item / merchant of record | ❌ | ✅ |
| Takes your payment | ❌ | ✅ |

| Provides order support and the official receipt | ❌ | ✅ |

Selv never handles your payment details, never takes title to the goods, and is not a party to your purchase contract. Your purchase is governed by the retailer's terms, privacy policy, and returns policy — read them.

**We earn a commission, and we say so in the app.** Selv is paid a percentage of the order value by the brand or its affiliate network when a purchase is attributed to a click from Selv. This costs you nothing extra: you pay the retailer's price either way. The same disclosure appears directly beneath the Buy button on every product page, before you tap it.

**How attribution works.** When you tap Buy, we create a record of that click and attach an opaque tracking identifier to the retailer's URL. If you complete a purchase, the retailer or its network reports it back to us so the sale can be attributed and the commission calculated. We do not receive your payment card details, your shipping address, or your account credentials with the retailer. What we receive is described in `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.

**Catalog information can be wrong or stale.** Prices, discounts, images, descriptions, available sizes, and stock levels are supplied by our partners through automated product feeds. They can be out of date or incorrect. **The retailer's own page at checkout is authoritative for price, availability, and size** — not Selv. We do not guarantee that any item shown in Selv is available, or available at the price shown.

**Your order history in Selv is not a receipt.** The Orders screen is assembled from what the retailer's system reports back to us. It can lag by days, can be missing an order entirely if the tracking identifier was lost in transit, and reflects our attribution pipeline rather than your order status. Your confirmation email from the retailer is the official record. Do not rely on Selv's Orders screen to prove a purchase, a price, a refund, or a return.

**Disputes about an order go to the retailer.** If an item never arrives, arrives damaged, is not as described, or you want to return it, contact the retailer. We are happy to help you find the right contact, but we cannot refund a purchase we did not take payment for.

## 17. Suspension, termination, and deleting your account

**You can leave at any time, from inside the app.** Profile → Delete account, then confirm. This is a real deletion, not a deactivation, and it satisfies [Apple's App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v)](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/). There is a confirmation step so a stray tap cannot delete your account by accident.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your character customization, any optional height and weight you entered, every garment photo you uploaded and its stored files, every garment record, every saved outfit, your wishlist, your try-on history, your Selv+ waitlist signup, and your sign-in credentials. `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` §10 describes the same flow from the privacy side, and `legal/DATA_HANDLING.md` §3 is the engineering specification for exactly what gets deleted and in what order.

**One narrow exception, stated plainly.** Records of affiliate clicks and of purchases attributed to them are **de-identified rather than deleted** — the link to you is removed, and the commercial record is kept. These rows are financial records: a conversion is money a brand owes, and the click is the evidence backing that invoice, including the commission rate agreed at the time. Deleting them would destroy the substantiation for invoices already issued and would make a later refund impossible to process. After de-identification the record no longer identifies you.

Copies of deleted data may persist briefly in encrypted backups before being purged, on the schedule in `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` §8.

Naming

Name shortlist

"Drobe" is already an active competitor — retire it. These encode the core mechanic (fit + digital twin) and lend themselves to Gen Z verbification. Run a 24-hour trademark/domain clearance before locking.

using here
Selv
"digital twin" + "we're twinning" (matching fits). selv.app likely open.
Fitcast
"fit" + broadcast your fit. Verbable — "fitcast it."
Mimic
Real word; literally what a parametric avatar does — mimics you.
Fitroom
"fitting room" collapsed. Safer, strong App Store keyword value.
Wardro
Short wardrobe-root, more ownable than "Drobe."

Body-image guardrail baked into the brand: the avatar is framed as a styling tool, never a body-evaluation tool. No numeric body/outfit scoring, reaction-based feedback only ("this ate," fire), diverse-body QA by default, and one-tap data deletion surfaced at photo upload. Copy hypes, never judges.
